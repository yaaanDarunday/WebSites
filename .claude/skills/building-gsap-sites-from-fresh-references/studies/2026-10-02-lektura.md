# Fresh study: lektura (premium stationery landing, centre die)

**Date:** 2026-10-02 · **Viewport:** 1440×900, DPR 1 · **Source:** gsap.com/showcase (495 cards harvested, 483 after ledger)
**Why these sites:** random 8-card shortlist (seeded shuffle), 7 carrying non-default plugins. Casa Lunara was parked (redirect to `ww38.`), and Komma Komma, House Of Yellow, Times Event and Pulse were dropped as photo/neon heroes with cookie walls that sit close to each other. The three kept differ on every axis:
- **SUTÉRA**: a white technical plate with one floating object in the centre and callout leader lines. Unlike any ledger build, the composition orbits a single object.
- **Durup**: a warm editorial textile studio. Vertical scroll drives a horizontal photo track under a frosted rail, and the display face is a high-contrast serif in caps.
- **Codapress**: a black technical publisher, all mono, with a draggable arc of book covers (Draggable + Inertia).

## 1. SUTÉRA
**Vibe:** a cool, quiet lab notebook. A single photographic object (a mossy rock with one red flower) hangs in the middle of a white page and is annotated like a specimen plate. It's calm and precise, a museum label meeting an engineering drawing.

### Layout and composition
- Faint hairline grid: verticals at x≈319 / 720 / 1120 and horizontals every ≈300px (y≈299, 599), with a **"+" crosshair at every intersection**. Grid tokens: `--grid-margin: 24px`, `--grid-gutter: 24px`.
- Headline top-left at 24px ("REALITY, BY DESIGN."). Wordmark top-left, a boxed "CHANGE REALITY" pill top-centre over a wireframe globe, "LOCAL TIME / ZUR 04:04 AM" top-right.
- Centre: the object at ≈470×400px, x 490–960, y 250–650, on a 738×415 canvas.
- **Four callouts** surround it. Each is a small 14px square node with a black fill that runs a 1px leader line (horizontal, a 45° jog, horizontal) out to an 11.7px uppercase caption. Positions are roughly NE, W, E and S.
- Bottom-left: a "[CORE THREADS OF MY WORK]" list of 4 rows separated by 1px rules. Bottom-right: a "NOT A STUDIO — JUST ME" card with an inverted black tab label and a chamfered corner.

### Typography
| Role | Font | Size / LH / tracking | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | NeueHaas 400 | 66.5px / 0.90 / −0.04em (`--text-xl: 4.62svw`) | uppercase hero; sentence case in body sections |
| Secondary display | NeueHaas 400 | 29.1px / 0.9 / −0.04em (`--text-xl-alt: 2.54svw`) | "Building foundations…" |
| Labels | PPNeueMontreal 400 | 11.7px / 1.1 / 0, uppercase | callouts, list rows |
| Micro | PPNeueMontreal 400 | 9.9–10px / 0.9–1.1, uppercase | indices "01.", "/25" |
| Section tag | NeueHaas 700 | 11.7px / 1.1 / −0.04em, uppercase | "[ CORE THREADS ]" |
All sizes are svw-fluid: `--text-m .92svw`, `--text-s .81svw`, `--text-10 max(10px,.57svw)`.

### Colour
`text #000` ×984, `border #000` ×18, `bg #fff`, a muted `#9e9e9e` for "LOCAL TIME", one `#cbcbcb` and one `#b5e9ff` (pale sky) tile. All chroma comes from the photographic objects. **No signal colour in the UI.**

### Motion
- Lenis (`html.lenis`), no CSS eases or durations (only `1ms ×1`). Motion is JS-driven *(inferred)*.
- The hero object **tilts toward the cursor**: after moving from centre to (1000,300) the rock's silhouette shifted and tilted (frames t0 and t1).
- The cursor is replaced by a small "+" box (class `no-cursor`), which inverts to a filled square over the leader-line nodes.
- Inline pictograms (sparkle, chain, eye, coral) sit inside the 66px sentences. Floating image cards sit beside the text with an "×" close button.
- "Some Project Examples" is a **scattered field of cut-out objects** numbered "01." to "09." at 11.7px, placed off-grid. The Draggable/Inertia card plugins suggest they can be thrown *(inferred)*.

### The one thing to steal
**An annotated specimen plate.** One object sits fixed in the centre of a faint "+" crosshair grid. Thin leader lines (horizontal, then a 45° jog, then horizontal) run from 14px square nodes on the object out to small uppercase captions, so the page reads like a labelled technical drawing of that one thing.

## 2. Durup
**Vibe:** a warm, slow, tactile Copenhagen interiors studio. Big saturated photographs of fabric and furniture slide past, while a frosted rail on the left keeps the voice quiet and grown-up.

### Layout and composition
- A fixed **left rail, 195px wide** (`--bar: 19.5rem` at the root size, `--bar-mobile: 11.5rem`), translucent `rgba(255,255,255,.15)` (`--lightwhite`) with a backdrop blur that smears the photograph scrolling beneath it.
- The rail holds MENU at the top, "Latest Project / Soho House Copenhagen" right-aligned at mid-height, and "→ EXPLORE" below.
- The rest is a horizontal track of full-height photographs at mixed widths (≈790px and ≈600px). The wordmark "DURUP" is fixed top-centre in white.

### Typography
| Role | Font | Size / LH / tracking | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | IvyPresto Display Light 400 | 64px / 0.969 / −0.003em, uppercase | the hero statement, centred |
| Sub-display | Futura PT Light | 34px / 0.941 / −0.006em, uppercase | "Explore our portfolio" |
| Body | Futura PT Book | 14–18px / 1.286 / −0.012em | rail text |
| Labels | Termina Demi | 10px / 1.4 / +0.025em, uppercase | MENU, EXPLORE |

### Colour
Root tokens: `--black #000`, `--white #fff`, `--lightergray #e6e6e6`, `--lightgray #999`, `--gray #5c5c5c`, `--red #a31a1c`, `--darkred #411808`. UI chroma is nearly zero; the photography carries it.

### Motion
- Native scroll. Vertical wheel moves the image track horizontally: on tick 2 the panels shifted ≈600px left *(inferred from mid2 vs set3)*.
- Root ease tokens include the full Penner set as cubic-béziers, for example `--ease-out-expo cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)` and `--ease-out-quint cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)`. Durations used: 0.15s, 0.2s, 0.3s, 0.4s and 0.8s.

### The one thing to steal
**A frosted rail.** A fixed, narrow side column of translucent white with `backdrop-filter: blur`, so whatever scrolls under it turns into a soft colour field while its small text stays perfectly still and readable.

## 3. Codapress
**Vibe:** a black, nerdy publisher of technical books. Everything is set in mono over a dithered checkerboard field. Book covers fan across the page like cards spread on a desk and can be thrown with the mouse.

### Layout and composition
- Full-bleed black page. A pill nav, 352×62px, is centred and goes translucent `#333` once you scroll.
- The hero is three lines of 56.5px uppercase mono, stepped and right-aligned, over a halftone/checker canvas.
- **The book carousel:** covers ≈270×360px sit along a gentle arc, each rotated a few degrees (left covers tilt −6°, right covers +6° *(inferred)*), with the title, a description and "View detail" below each.
- The cursor turns into a 112×112px `#48ff00` square reading "‹ DRAG ›" over the carousel.

### Typography
| Role | Font | Size / LH | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | JetBrains Mono 400 | 56.5px / 46.4px (0.82), uppercase | hero |
| H2 | JetBrains Mono 400 | 37.7px / 33.9px | "Latest release" |
| Body | JetBrains Mono 400 | 24px / 26.4px | lede |
| Card | JetBrains Mono 700 / 400 | 11.3px / 12.4–13.5px | titles, descriptions |
| Micro | JetBrains Mono 400 | 9.8px | "Bestseller" |

### Colour
`--color-bg #000`, `--color-text #fff`, `--color-mid-grey #8F8F8F`, `--color-muted #575757`, `--color-btn #333`, and the single hot signal `--cursor-hover-color #48ff00`. Fluid type uses `lvw` clamps, for example `--text-display clamp(32px, 3.571lvw, 137px)`.

### Motion
- Lenis: 79% of a tick covered at 250ms, settled at 1.7s.
- Draggable + Inertia carousel: covers keep their arc-tilt relationship as the strip moves *(inferred from the set1/set2 rotation spread)*.
- The "Latest release" frame is an empty 1330×630 box with a centred square loader until media streams in.

### The one thing to steal
**An arc-fanned throwable strip.** Cards sit along a shallow arc, and each one's rotation and vertical drop come from its distance to the viewport centre. Dragging with Draggable + Inertia throws the strip, and every card re-tilts live as it travels, like spreading cards across a desk.

## 4. Across these sites
- **Shared, and absent from the ledger:** each page is organised around one **physical artefact**: a rock specimen, fabric photographs, book covers. None of them uses a signal colour as UI chrome except Codapress's cursor. None splits or scrambles its headlines. Captions are tiny uppercase sans (SUTÉRA, Durup), not mono, in two of the three.
