## 1. San Rita
**Vibe:** An expedition field map for a creative studio, like a national-park trail guide drawn by a skate-brand designer. It is cool and outdoorsy with a wink of adventure. Calm ground, then a glowing trail you want to follow.

### Layout and composition
- **Framed stage.** A photographic terrain map sits in a box from x≈390 to 1385 and y≈40 to 860. The page ground around it is icy blue `#dde2e4`.
- Hairline **4-column grid lines** cross the frame and extend past it into the margin, like survey lines.
- **Left rail:** "THE TRAILS OF SAN RITA" (10px mono bold caps), then the nav as a **map legend**. Each item has a small black pictogram (map, campfire, cabin, mushroom, flag): Map, Projects, About, Playground, Contact, Instagram, LinkedIn. Below it is a real legend: Road, Trail, Fishing spot, Viewpoint, each with its own glyph. Then EN/FR.
- **Top ruler:** a sun glyph, a live clock "20:30:32 GMT", "EST.2024" and a compass glyph, all sitting on the grid lines.
- **Vertical side captions** in 10px mono with 0.3em tracking: "DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT PURVEYORS" and "DIGITAL DESIGN REFUGE HAND-CRAFTED".
- **Bottom:** a scale bar "72PX | 54PT" (left) and "16.1KM | 10MI" (right), plus centred taglines ("GOLD IDEAS SEEKERS", "REPUBLIC OF COLLABORATIVE MINDS").
- **Hero statement** bottom-left in condensed caps: "A CREATIVE STUDIO WHERE BRANDS AND STORIES MOVE OFF-TRAILS".
- A dark "LATEST HOT SPOT ADDED" card bottom-right with a project name and image.
- Map zoom controls (+/−) on the right edge.

### Typography
| Role | Font | Size / LH / Tracking | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero statement | **F37 Stout** 400 uppercase | 56px / 0.9 / 0 | Very condensed, heavy |
| Card title | F37 Stout 400 uppercase | 48px / 0.9 / 0 | "PODIUM GLOBAL" |
| Nav / legend | mono 400 uppercase | 12px / normal / 0 | ×16 |
| Rail title | mono 700 uppercase | 10px | "THE TRAILS OF SAN RITA" |
| Side captions | mono 400 uppercase | 10px / **+0.3em** | Set vertically |
| Micro | mono 400 uppercase | 8px / 1.25 | "VIEW MORE" |
| Logotype | Hand-drawn brush SVG | — | "SAN RITA", lime |

### Colour
Named tokens: `--color-icy-blue: #dde2e4` (ground), `--color-forest-green: #2d3329` (ink), `--color-terrain-grey: #84907f` (secondary text), `--color-adventure-yellow: #e2ffcc` (trails, hero text on the map, buttons), `--color-deep-green: #161b13`. Chroma comes from the terrain photo (rust ridges, green valleys). **No saturated signal colour.** The "highlight" is a pale luminous lime.

### Motion
- Preloader (about 10s): "TERRAFORMING ACTIVATED" with two columns of status lines ("DATA CALCULATION… ATMOSPHERE DEPLOYMENT…", "WHISPER OF DATA AWAKENED…"). A trail with nodes draws itself in the centre and a percentage counter runs ("_83%").
- First wheel: the framed map **zooms to full-bleed**. The frame, rail and ruler disappear. Street names ("ADRIAN PROVINCIAL PARK", "THENPONT RIVER") appear rotated along the terrain, and the hero text turns lime over the map.
- Trails are thick lime polylines with dark circular nodes. Hovering a node pops a **glowing radial grid halo** and a label ("ABOUT") above the icon.
- Eases: `cubic-bezier(0, 0.55, 0.45, 1)` (out-circ), an overshoot `cubic-bezier(0.18, 0.89, 0.32, 1.28)` and `cubic-bezier(0, 0.57, 0.46, 2)`. Durations 0.15–0.5s, and 1s.
- Keyframes: `pulse`, `next-trail-arrow`, `flicker`, `shake`, `reveal-scroll-content-x/y`, and page transitions `transition-scale-in/out`, `transition-mask-in/out`.

### The one thing to steal
**The site is a map and the nav is its legend.** Content lives at nodes on a trail; moving through the site means travelling the trail.

## 2. Sui Overflow 2026
**Vibe:** A hackathon brochure as a sticker-bomb toybox. Chunky 3D keycaps spell OVERFLOW over a blue graph-paper grid. It is bright, loud and friendly, but the type stays disciplined.

### Layout and composition
- **Top bar** is split into two cells: title left, and a dark `#000f1d` cell right holding "`<date>` May – August, 2026 `</date>`" with the code tags in blue mono. The cells **swap light and dark as sections change**.
- **Bottom tab bar**, fixed, with 7 equal cells bordered in 1px: Overview, Why join us?, Tracks, Prizes, Sponsors, FAQ, and a dark "View Projects" cell with a blue arrow cell. **The active tab fills with that section's colour:** Overview is blue `#4da2ff`, Why join is yellow `#ffd731`, Tracks is orange `#ff7a00`.
- Hero: a 140px headline on the left; on the right an illustrated keycap cluster that stays put while the headline scrolls away.
- Cards are built from a **visible cell grid**: 1px bordered squares around each content bar, with a small coloured number tag ("01", "02"…) in mono.
- Section grounds alternate: light `#f7f7f7`, then navy `#000f1d`, then cream `#f2eee4`.

### Typography
| Role | Font | Size / LH / Tracking |
|---|---|---|
| Display | **TWK Everett** 500 | 140px / 0.85 / −0.05em |
| Section title | TWK Everett 500 | 120px / 0.85 / −0.05em |
| Big stat | TWK Everett 500 | 64px / 1.0 / −0.05em |
| Tabs / UI | TWK Everett 500 | 18px / 1.2 / −0.05em |
| Tags | TWK Everett Mono 400 | 12px / 1.0 |

### Colour
Navy ink `#000f1d`, light `#f7f7f7`, cream `#f2eee4`, plus a **full toy palette** used per section and per tag: blue `#4da2ff`, deepskyblue, green `#55db9c` / `#4df766`, yellow `#ffd731`, orange `#ff7a00`, purple `#5c4ade`, pink `#e9ccff` / `#ff6ada`.

### Motion
- 207 ScrollTriggers, 0 pins and 8 scrubs: lots of small reveals rather than set pieces.
- Default token `--animation-default: 0.525s cubic-bezier(0.525, 0, 0, 1)`, plus a back-out `cubic-bezier(0.175, 0.885, 0.32, 1.175)`.
- Keyframes: `popCenter`, `popBottom`, `slideTop`, `marqueeScroll`, `speechDotsAnimation`, `footerBlinker`.
- Content skeletons (grey bars) shimmer in before the text arrives.

### The one thing to steal
**A bottom tab bar where the active tab fills with the current section's colour.** It is wayfinding and a palette showcase at once.

## 3. Zeit Media
**Vibe:** A loud event-production house. A wall of saturated Vietnamese event posters sits under a clean white grotesk on pure black. It is confident and maximal in its imagery, minimal in its type.

### Layout and composition
- Hero: a **dense poster wall** of 4 columns of tall posters with 30px gaps, scaled (`matrix(0.9…)`) and scrolling. A white 80px headline "Art Comes First — Creative Aboves All" sits over it bottom-left, and a "NEW PROJECT" card sits bottom-right.
- Stats row (200+, 1000+, 200+) at 112px over a darkened event photo.
- **Client list as one justified 48px uppercase paragraph**, centred and wrapped to fill the width (×272 spans, one per word).

### Typography
**Interdisplay** only: 160px/500 "Be different." (the largest), 136px/600 uppercase at 0.8 line-height, 112px stats, 48px/600 uppercase client wall with −0.03em tracking, and 20px/700 captions.

### Colour
Pure black `rgb(0,0,0)` with white. All chroma comes from the poster imagery.

### Motion
- GSAP 3.13 with ScrollTrigger and Flip, 14 triggers, Lenis.
- CSS transitions on `cubic-bezier(0.625, 0.05, 0, 1)` ×24. That's the same "osmo" curve as Revelatio (ledger), so it isn't new.

### The one thing to steal
The justified wall of names used as a typographic texture.

## 4. Across these sites
- None use `mix-blend-mode: difference`. Only Sui uses a mono, and only for tags.
- San Rita avoids a saturated signal colour entirely; Sui uses many colours as a system. **Both break the ledger's "neutral pair plus one signal colour" habit.**
- San Rita and Sui both treat **navigation as an object inside the design** (a map legend, a colour-filling tab bar), not as a text row in the header.

| Site | Temperature | Energy | Personality | Signature |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| San Rita | Cool-earthy | Calm, exploratory | Outdoorsy, playful | Map UI, trail nodes, legend nav |
| Sui Overflow | Bright, cool | High, bouncy | Toybox, friendly | Section-coloured tab bar, cell grids |
| Zeit Media | Hot imagery on black | High | Loud, commercial | Poster wall, justified name wall |
