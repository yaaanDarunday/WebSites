## 1. Andres Briganti
**Vibe:** Cool, quiet and confident. It reads like a type specimen pinned to a studio wall: a near-white sheet, tiny black labels, and the work doing all the talking. There is low UI energy but a continuous drift, because the wall never ends.

### Layout and composition
- A 12-column grid with a 24px gap (`--spacing-gap: 24/16em`) and 12px sides (`--spacing-sides`).
- The tiles are 222px wide in 4 columns at x = 9 / 369 / 729 / 1089 on even rows and x = 129 / 489 / 849 / 1209 on odd rows. Each row shifts by 120px, which gives a checkerboard stagger.
- Tile heights mix 222 (square), 296 (3:4) and 167 (4:3). The row pitch is about 435px, and tiles are offset vertically inside a row (y 90 / 123 / 90 / 101).
- A vertical wordmark "BRIGANTI" runs the left edge, 98px wide by 900 tall, rotated 90°. It clips and loops with the wall.
- Fixed text sits in one row at y ≈ 454 ("Version / MM25.1.1 / Brand Identity & Visual Communication / bio"), aligned to the same grid columns. **The tiles pass over the text:** the bio is occluded mid-glide.
- Header: "Selection" at top-left, Home/Info/Work at top-right, and "© MM26" at bottom-right.

### Typography
| Role | Font | Size / LH / tracking | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | custom heavy grotesk SVG | 98px wide by 900 tall | Vertical and rotated; it is the only big type |
| Info headings | Roma Neue 500 | 21px / 1.4 / 0, uppercase | Info view only |
| Labels, nav, bio | Roma Neue 500 | 10.5px / 1.2 / 0 | ×63. Everything is this size |
| Section label | Inferi 450 | 11.3px / 1.1 | "Selection" |

The type is almost entirely **one size, 10.5px**, plus one giant vertical wordmark. There are no middle sizes and no mono.

### Colour
- Ink `rgb(23,22,21)` `#171615` ×132, paper `rgb(250,250,250)` `#fafafa` ×98.
- No signal colour. All chroma comes from the work images (red poster, blue sky, lime blob).
- The nav is "Home" in ink with the inactive "Info"/"Work" in grey, reading as about 35% alpha *(from screenshot)*.

### Motion
- **Scroll model:** the Observer glides the wall. A 100px wheel moves a tile 100px with exponential decay. Sampled every 100ms, `y` went −421, −444, −460, −467, −473, −475, −477, −478, −478: about 90% done by 400ms and settled by about 700ms (a lerp of about 0.2 per frame). The wall wraps infinitely, and by the third tick the first row reappears below.
- No ScrollTriggers, no pins, no Lenis.
- CSS eases: `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)` ×6 @0.15s (UI), `cubic-bezier(0.51,0,0.1,1)` ×3 @0.65s, `cubic-bezier(0.19,1,0.22,1)` ×1 @1s. Keyframes: `slideUp`, `slideDown`.
- `mix-blend-mode: difference` is used on the fixed header, footer and the wall layer (so labels invert over tiles).
- Hover on a tile: no transform, filter or opacity change was measured.

### The one thing to steal
**An infinite, wrapping wall of staggered work tiles that the wheel glides (lerped, about 700ms settle) under a fixed layer of tiny labels.** The page itself never scrolls. The wall has 4 columns, every other row shifts half a column, and the tiles occlude the fixed text as they pass.

## 2. Sublimio
**Vibe:** Neutral-dark and hushed, like a naming agency whispering its pitch. It is a single typeface in two greys, with big media frames that open up as you arrive.

### Layout and composition
- One sentence sits at x = 292 on an otherwise empty 900px black hero.
- Work frames start inset (x 243 to 1197, 954px wide) and widen to x 80 to 1360 (1280px) as they scroll into view.

### Typography
| Role | Font | Size / LH / tracking | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero sentence | TT Hoves Regular 400 | 44px / 1.23 / −0.14px | Line 1 `#e3e3e3`, lines 2–3 `#999999` |
| Nav | TT Hoves Regular 400 | 18px / 1.01 / −0.06px | `#5e5e5e` |

### Colour
Background `#000000`, which becomes `#191919` (body `rgb(25,25,25)`) once scrolled. Text `#e3e3e3` / `#999999` / `#5e5e5e`. No signal colour.

### Motion
- Locomotive-style transform scroll. At +250ms after a tick the content had moved about 110px, and it had moved about 220px by +1.7s, so it is heavily smoothed.
- **The headline tail rotates:** "We create surprising names for memorable brands." became "We create strong brands for a tough world." between loads and ticks.
- Eases: 85× `ease` @0.5s, custom `cubic-bezier(0.84,0.01,0.2,1.01)` ×2, and an overshoot `cubic-bezier(0.485,0.155,0.24,1.245)`.

### The one thing to steal
**A two-tone sentence whose second half cycles through alternatives.** The first clause is fixed in light grey, and the tail is swapped in mid-grey on a timer.

## 3. Oaksun Studio
**Vibe:** Warm, playful and polished. It pairs pastel paper `#e4e5de` and salmon `#f6ada9` with an iridescent 3D ribbon video, a huge rounded heavy display face, and pill buttons.

### Layout, type, colour
- HKGrotesk at every size: nav 20px/300, pills 20px/500, the "WEB DESIGN + DEV STUDIO" kicker 36px/700 white, and the display "OAKSUN" at about 300px.
- Ink `#0f0f0f` ×50, white ×35, paper `rgb(228,229,222)`, salmon `rgb(246,173,169)`, and a header that turns coral with teal on scroll.

### Motion
- Native scroll: scrollY hit exactly 750, 1500, 2250 and so on at +250ms, with no smoothing.
- A giant "WORK" marquee sits on black with a "Swipe" pill (Draggable plus Inertia per the card).
- **Stacked work cards:** each 905px card sticks near the top, and the previous card scales down and darkens behind it.
- A cursor-follow "Open" pill sits over the cards.

### The one thing to steal
Sticky cards that stack, where the previous card recedes (scale about 0.97, dimmed) under the next.

## 4. Across these sites
- **What they share that the ledger's builds don't:**
  - None uses a mono.
  - Two of the three use **one typeface family** for everything.
  - None has a signal colour, so the chroma comes from the work.
  - Briganti and Sublimio both whisper: body and labels at 10–18px, and no split-letter headline reveals.
- **Vibe matrix:**

  | Site | Temperature | Energy | Personality | Signature |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Briganti | cool-neutral | low UI, constant drift | specimen wall | infinite wrapping tile wall |
  | Sublimio | neutral-dark | hushed | whispering agency | rotating phrase, widening frames |
  | Oaksun | warm pastel | playful | toy-like, polished | iridescent ribbon, stacking cards |
